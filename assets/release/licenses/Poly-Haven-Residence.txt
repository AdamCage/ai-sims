The glTF model resources were repacked as single GLB containers. Dining
table, lounge chair, bench and potted-plant textures were resized to at
most 512 pixels per side for the game camera. The potted plant meshes
were simplified in Blender from 176,226 to 26,763 triangles, preserving
the original material assignments. Architecture, fixtures and tree
mesh arrangements are original AI Sims artwork.
